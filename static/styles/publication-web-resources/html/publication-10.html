KIM PIFFY uses typefaces SOURCED FROM Velvetyne Type Foundry.

Velvetyne makes professionally designed, visually distinctive typefaces available for

under open licences, while maintaining a clear culture of authorship and attribution.

This makes the foundry especially appropriate for a practice concerned with

COMMUNITY AND CO-CREATION, AND WHICH CHAMPIONS alternatives to restrictive or extractive systems.

The typefaces are freely available, but they do not feel generic or compromised. They

offer strong character, experimentation and formal quality without placing

professional typography behind a financial barrier.

aCROSS THE SITE HEADERS AND BUTTONS USE LOWERCASE IN A DORMANT STATE, WHICH TRANSFORMS TO UPPERCASE WHEN ACTIVATED. ALL HEADERS USE A STROKE PATH AND GLOW TO REFERENCE KIM’S LIGHT BASED PRACTICE, BEGINNING IN URANIUM WITH A PINK STROKE PATH, AND INVERTING ON

ACTIVATION.
