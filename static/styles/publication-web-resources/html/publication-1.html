Kim piffy is a multidisciplinary artist and web designer working across digital and physical spaces. Her work combines conceptual thinking with a playful visual language, often using colour, texture, light and interactive elements to create environments that are sensory and immersive.

Flourescence is a key material reference and centrifugal to kims artistic practice. The challenge has been to give the site a luminous and synthetic quality that will effectivley connect Kim’s digital portfolio to the atmospheric qualities of her physical work. The aesthetic is intentionally playful without becoming childish, and slightly strange without becoming inaccessible.

the result is a site that is certainly not lacking in kim’s unique expression whilst still being comfortably held within a clear navigational system. The site is an expression of Kim’s practice; imaginative and experimental with a respectful recognition for how different people may enter and experience the space.
