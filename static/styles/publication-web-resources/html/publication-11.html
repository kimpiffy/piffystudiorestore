FOOTER

The footer is intentionally minimal, fixed in place, and consistently accessible across the site. It contains only essential elements: a direct, copy-and-paste email address, site credit, navigation, and social text links. This stripped-back approach prioritises clarity and permanence over decoration. By fixing the footer, these functions remain available at all times without requiring the user to search or scroll, reinforcing ease of access and reducing friction. The use of plain text links—rather than icons or stylised components—keeps interaction unambiguous and avoids unnecessary visual noise. The visible email address, in particular, removes layers of mediation, offering a direct and reliable method of contact that exists outside of controlled interfaces. Altogether, the footer operates as a stable, functional base layer of the site: quiet, persistent, and intentionally resistant to over-design.
