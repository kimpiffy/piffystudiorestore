BRAND

IDENTITY

Rather than repeatedly applying a flat logo across the site, the brand identity is established through light, colour and movement. Video footage of Kim’s neon logo translates an existing physical artwork into the digital space, allowing the identity to feel atmospheric rather than imposed. This approach was informed by contemporary portfolio and institutional websites, including Louis Vuitton and Falmouth University, where moving image helps to IMMEDIATELY establish identity without relying on a conventional logo placement.
